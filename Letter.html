Happy 520 Day!
Congratulations to everyone who guessed the correct album title: Secret of 5:20 a.m.

Today, I am releasing the prelude-intro version, which includes song introductions for "Forever Yours" and "Good Boy!!!". I wrote, composed, recorded, and produced these songs entirely in my studio. However, there were times when I recorded my vocals on the spot whenever inspiration struck.

Many people have asked me why I haven’t released the album yet. The reason is that while I was recording, I was secretly preparing a massive surprise for all of you. Bringing this surprise to life has made my schedule much busier and harder. The scale of this surprise is truly massive—the workload feels triple that of my debut. So, please wait patiently; it will be released very soon.

"Forever Yours" is the fourth track of the album, and it is the deepest, saddest song I have ever made. I originally began working on this track when I was twelve years old. The song tells the story of a man who falls deeply in love, but due to circumstances beyond his control, the relationship ends. Unable to accept the loss, he ultimately takes his own life; the lyrics serve as his final note. To me, this story is deeply personal. I met a girl when I was twelve, and we fell in love, but an unfair situation forced us apart. Even though it brought me immense heartbreak, I tried desperately to force myself to move on, but I couldn't. I wrote a poem to process the pain, and turning it into this song took three years—a period of time that was incredibly exhausting and painful. Now that the song is finally finished, I hope to release it very soon.

"Good Boy!!!" is the sixth track. The lyrics are raw, intense, and provocative. The song explores themes of power and control—the idea that when you hold the power, you can get anything you want, especially love. For a long time, I tried so hard to be a "good boy," but it only left me feeling sad and broken. The song touches on the confusing, conflicting feeling of being overwhelmed by external forces while wishing for it to stop:

"Although I enjoy this, I want to stop."

I channeled all of this bewilderment into a five-minute writing session to produce this track.

Happy 520 to all of you. I hope you have a wonderful day filled with energy and romance.
